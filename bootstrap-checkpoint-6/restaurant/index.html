About Us

Where Every Meal Tells a Story

Founded in 2012 by chef Amelie Laurent, Saveur began as a small family kitchen with one simple belief: food is memory. Today, our candle-lit dining room welcomes guests from all over the city to enjoy dishes inspired by French countryside cooking with a modern twist.

We work directly with local farmers and fishermen to source the freshest seasonal ingredients, and every sauce, bread, and dessert is made in-house, from scratch, every single day.

  • Award-winning head chef
  • Farm-to-table seasonal ingredients
  • Warm, family-friendly atmosphere
